## Brand & Style
The brand ethos merges high-velocity deep technology with an immersive, atmospheric visual hierarchy. Targeted at developers, crypto-native operators, and visionary engineers, the interface evokes the quiet focus of an advanced command console operating in deep space. 

Visual style fuses deep futuristic minimalism with controlled, luminous neon luminescence. By restricting the chromatic spectrum entirely to high-frequency violets, dark indigos, and electric purples against an obsidian-purple void, the interface eliminates extraneous noise. Surfaces feel layered, tactile, and reactive without becoming chaotic. Precise geometric discipline grounds the aesthetic, balancing futuristic flair with enterprise-grade utility.

## Colors
The palette relies on a strictly curated spectrum of rich violets, purples, and deep-space blacks. No conflicting warm hues (orange, pink) or cool deviations (teal, cyan) are permitted.

- **Background Canvas (`#0B0518`)**: Deep dark purple-black serving as the foundational cosmic void.
- **Card & Container Layer (`#1A0B2E`)**: Dark violet-tinted surface establishing physical separation from the base canvas.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#2E1A4D`)**: Low-frequency structural lines defining component boundaries without harsh contrast.
- **Primary Color (`#7C3AED`)**: Royal purple used for primary actions, prominent status badges, and interactive selections.
- **Secondary / Neon Glow (`#A855F7`)**: High-energy electric violet used for active states, key indicators, and radiant ambient backlights.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#C084FC`)**: Soft lavender accent for badges, tertiary highlights, and interactive toggles.
- **Primary Typography (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure optic white engineered for maximum legibility and high contrast across all dark layers.
- **Secondary Typography (`#D8B4FE`)**: Muted light lavender providing effortless hierarchical separation for metadata, supporting copy, and passive icons.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm contrasts the forward-looking geometry of Sora for prominent headlines with the utilitarian rigor of Inter for dense information readouts. 

- **Headlines (Sora)**: Designed with a technical, high-aperture structure that brings an immediate technological presence to heroes, module headers, and section leads.
- **Body & Labels (Inter)**: Delivers uncompromising clarity at small scales, supporting heavy density data visualization, tables, and settings without visual strain.
- **Scale Rules**: Headings above 32px automatically compress via mobile token counterparts to maintain comfortable reading lines and avoid awkward line breaks on compact displays.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`min-width: 1024px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet (`min-width: 768px`) and a 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Margins**: 2rem (`32px`) on wide screens, stepping down to 1rem (`16px`) on mobile viewports to conserve interactive real estate.
- **Gutters**: 1.5rem (`24px`) on desktop for breathing room between dense functional modules; 1rem (`16px`) on smaller breakpoints.
- **Rhythm**: Internal spacing relies strictly on multiples of 4px and 8px. Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for inline grouping within badges, chips, and input bars; apply `space-md` and `space-lg` for module cards and modal interiors; deploy `space-xl` to segment primary dashboard domains.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is constructed through luminance stacking and controlled photonic radiation rather than conventional opaque drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - *Base Layer*: Canvas background (`#0B0518`).
  - *Mid Layer*: Containers and cards (`#1A0B2E`) with a 1px continuous border of `#2E1A4D`.
  - *Top Layer*: Modals, flyouts, and active dialogs (`#24103F`) with border `#8B5CF6` at 30% opacity.
- **Luminescence & Glows**:
  - Interactive highlights and focus states emit an electric aura using `#A855F7` with blur radii ranging between 12px and 24px at 20% to 35% opacity.
  - Primary button hover states utilize a concentrated glow: `0 0 20px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.45)`.
- **Ghost Depth**: Deep inset border overlays provide an etched, structural aesthetic, avoiding flat monotony while ensuring the dark canvas retains depth.

## Shapes
A roundedness factor of 2 establishes a balanced geometry: smooth enough to feel refined and tactile, yet sharp enough to maintain a technical, futuristic silhouette.

- **Standard Base (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to buttons, input fields, dropdown triggers, and interactive list items.
- **Large Surfaces (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Applied to panels, metric cards, dashboards, and dialog modules.
- **Extra Large Containers (`1.5rem` / `24px`)**: Applied to primary modal views, elevated canvas regions, and notification trays.
- **Full Radius**: Reserved strictly for micro-status indicators, badges, and contextual pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#7C3AED`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `transparent`, shape `0.5rem`. On hover, background shifts to `#8B5CF6` accompanied by an active glow: `0 0 20px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background `transparent`, border 1px solid `#2E1A4D`, text `#D8B4FE`. On hover, border activates to `#A855F7`, text transitions to `#FFFFFF`, and background receives a `#1A0B2E` wash.
- **Ghost**: Background `transparent`, text `#D8B4FE`. On hover, text `#FFFFFF` with `#1A0B2E` underlay.

### Chips & Badges
- Background `#1A0B2E`, 1px border of `#2E1A4D`, typography `label-sm` in `#C084FC`.
- **Active / Neon Variant**: Background `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`, border 1px solid `#A855F7`, text `#FFFFFF` with an ambient glow indicator dot.

### Lists & Tables
- Table headers use `label-md` in `#D8B4FE`, uppercase with `0.05em` letter spacing, sitting on base canvas `#0B0518`.
- Rows use subtle separation via 1px bottom borders in `#2E1A4D`. Alternating hover states introduce a smooth transition to `#1A0B2E`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Inactive state: 1px border in `#2E1A4D` over `#0B0518`, rounded to `0.25rem` (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Active state: `#7C3AED` solid fill with `#FFFFFF` inner mark and a 4px soft outer aura tinted with `#A855F7`.

### Input Fields
- Container fill: `#1A0B2E`, border: 1px solid `#2E1A4D`, typography: `body-md` in `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder text rests in `#D8B4FE` at 50% opacity.
- Focused state: Border transitions to `#8B5CF6` with a ring shadow of `0 0 0 2px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.25)`.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from `#1A0B2E` with 1px structural framing of `#2E1A4D`.
- Featured or key metric modules gain an illuminated top border accent in `#8B5CF6` or subtle radial gradient wash centered at the top perimeter.

### Terminal & Metric Displays
- High-density data readout cards featuring monospaced metrics in `#FFFFFF` with neon telemetry beacons (`#A855F7`) displaying live network updates.
